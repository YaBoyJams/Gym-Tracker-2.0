# GYM Tracker 2.0 Concept

## Purpose
The purpose of the tracker is so that the end user can input the exercise they just finished, the exercise name, the weight of the exercise, the amount of reps and sets, and the muscle/muscle group hit.

## Rules
AI will be used only as a teacher/mentor rule, guiding me and helping me learn throughout this project and reviewing my code.
AI will not write code, just give out explinations and help explain concepts to me unless specifically stated ourwise.

## Core Functionality
- Selecting a muscle group
- Selecting a specific muscle
- Selecting the exercise
- Setting the weight used, the amount of reps and sets
- Save inputted workout
- Ability to load previous workouts
- Ability to compare current workout vs most recently logged one
- Ability to log PRs

## Planned Structure

- ExerciseMenu
- MuscleGroup
- Muscle
- Exercise
- FileManager
